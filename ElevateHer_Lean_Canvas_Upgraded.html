🛡 Unfair Advantage 09
Proprietary female-career dataset
World Bank Women in Work + 3 pilot school cohorts = 2,400 labelled career paths. Impossible to replicate without same female-specific training data.
Data flywheel moat
More matches → better match quality → more girls → more mentors. Self-reinforcing; gap widens vs. competitors over time.
SMS/USSD-first architecture
No competitor operates offline for SE Asia. First-mover in 2G-compatible mentorship — 2.4B addressable users invisible to app-first rivals.
SDG-aligned institutional trust
UN, World Bank, CSR alignment attracts grant funding competitors can't access. GoGlobal partner network = 6 institutions as launch distribution.
Moderated trust layer
Verified mentors + NLP bias screening + guardian consent — regulatory moat that generic platforms cannot retrofit quickly.
